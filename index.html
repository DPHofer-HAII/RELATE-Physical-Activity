RELATE: Personalised AI Support for Physical Activity

We're exploring how AI-generated motivational messages, tailored to your context and daily experience, can help you move more over four weeks.

What is RELATE about?

Most of us want to be more active. But wanting and doing are two very different things. RELATE is a four-week research study investigating whether AI-generated messages, personalised to your daily context and individual profile, can make a meaningful difference to your physical activity, and how you feel about it.

The study runs across four parts:

  • Part 1 — Getting started (Day 1): A questionnaire about you, your habits, and your goals — plus setting up your personal schedule: which days you plan to be active and a preferred time window for your daily message. Takes around 30-45 minutes.
  • Part 2 — The active phase (Days 2–29): On your scheduled days, a short questionnaire and a personalised motivational message will arrive at the start of your chosen time window. Answering the questionnaire should take approx. 6 minutes, and you have until the end of your chosen time frame to respond. Each evening, on a day chosen for PA, at 9 pm, a brief check-in (approx. 5 minutes) asks whether you followed through on your plans. Every seven days, you'll update your schedule and answer a short follow-up (~5 minutes).
  • Part 3 — Wrapping up (Day 30): A closing questionnaire about your overall experience (~30-45 minutes). To be eligible, you'll need at least 12 successfully completed days; Meaning, you both evaluated your motivational message and submitted the evening check-in.
  • Optional Part 4 — Exit interview: A one-hour video conversation about your experience (limited places). Available to those who complete Parts 1–3.
Is RELATE right for you? RELATE is designed for people who want to be more physically active but sometimes struggle with motivation or getting started. If you're already training daily or competing in sport, this study probably isn't your best fit. But if you're somewhere in between "I should move more" and "where do I even begin," we'd love to have you. We'll confirm your activity level with a brief questionnaire during onboarding.

Total time commitment: approximately 3.5 hours across 30 days (4.5 hours if you join the optional interview).

About the Messages

Your daily motivational messages are generated by a Large Language Model (LLM), an AI system, drawing on contextual information you share each day (mood, stress, motivation, perceived barriers, PA intention) alongside your onboarding profile. These messages are designed as personalised prompts to support your motivation, not as professional health or fitness advice. As with any AI-generated content, outputs may occasionally miss the mark. We encourage you to use your own judgement.

Compensation

As a thank-you for your time, you will receive €50.00 (approx. CAD $79, exchange rate dependent) upon completing the study. If you take part in the optional exit interview, compensation increases to €65.00 (approx. CAD $103).

Compensation requires a minimum of 12 successfully completed days and completion of the post-study questionnaire. A "successful day" means completing both the intervention questionnaire (scheduled by you) and the evening check-in on a day you scheduled physical activity. Compensation is disbursed at the close of the study.

Your Rights & Withdrawal

Participation is entirely voluntary — you are free to withdraw at any time, for any reason, without penalty. All research data is collected in pseudonymised, GDPR-compliant form. By signing up, you confirm that you are 18 or older, fluent in English, and have read and understood how your data will be used (see the Data Protection section below).

Data Protection & Security

Information about how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Data We Collect

  • Pre-study questionnaire responses (attitudes toward physical activity, trust in AI, current activity level, demographics)
  • Weekly physical activity schedule (which days you plan to be active, preferred message time windows)
  • Contextual input before each message (mood, stress, self-efficacy, motivation, perceived barriers to PA)
  • Post-message assessments (perceived empathy, relevance, helpfulness, affective response)
  • End-of-day check-ins (whether you completed your planned activity)
  • Weekly follow-up questionnaires (attitudes toward physical activity)
  • Post-study questionnaire (overall system experience, Trust in AI)
  • Optional exit interview recordings and transcripts (for selected participants only)
  • Email address for study communication and compensation delivery

How We Use Your Data

  • Generate personalised motivational messages using an AI language model based on your questionnaire responses and contextual input
  • Conduct research analysis for scientific publications and conference presentations
  • Improve understanding of personalisation in digital health interventions
  • All published results will be anonymised — no identifying information will be disclosed

Data Storage & Security

  • All data stored on a local, institution-managed server at the Ludwig Boltzmann Institute
  • Data is pseudonymised using unique participant IDs — personal identifiers kept separate from research data
  • The ID-to-name mapping document (required for reimbursement) is accessible only to the principal investigator and permanently deleted after compensation is disbursed
  • Access limited to authorised research team members only
  • Data retained for 10 years per Austrian research data retention guidelines, then securely deleted

Your Rights as a Participant

  • Participation is completely voluntary
  • Right to withdraw from active participation at any time without penalty
  • All research data is collected in pseudonymised form using participant IDs
  • Right to file complaints with the Austrian Data Protection Authority
